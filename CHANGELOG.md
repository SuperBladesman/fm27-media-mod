# Changelog

## v0.9.2-beta (24th November 2026)

Three downloads from now on: the Media Mod, and the first betas of its two sister mods, the **Treatment Room** and **Partnerships**. Each works on its own.

### The Media Mod

- **The Press Office** (Press Room 0.15.1; the Media Office until now) has a new look and a front page, **This week**:
  - the pressure on you, with your league position and last five results;
  - the next press conference, and which journalist is likely to come after you, and why;
  - your press office's hours, the jobs on your desk and the stories running;
  - how the press room, the fans and the dressing room feel about you.

  Every other tab has the new look too.
- **Contract talks aren't transfers.** Talks over a new contract for one of your own players now read as renewals in the whispers, the press and the deals list, not as a signing. Earlier renewals in a save are refiled when it loads.
- **How they took it.** After a chat or a team meeting, the Dressing room tab records your line and the room's reaction ("16 Downcast, 11 Unimpressed"), with how the room felt before.
- A brief back against an agent now waits for a question it fits.
- With the sister mods installed, the press raise their stories (at most one of each per press conference): a recurring injury, a squad stretched by injuries, the department short of staff, a partnership split up or back together, a new pair starting to click. In The Terraces, fans now and then talk about a partnership under the match stories (Tabloid 0.6.8).
- **One column of buttons on the right-hand edge** (new: Core 0.2.4). The Press Office, the Treatment Room and Partnerships each get a button, stacked with Heffem's Academy Manager hub if you have it. A short note pops out beside a button when there's something worth a look, and opens the right page. The buttons show only in a game, not on the start screens.
- **A fact sheet** (new: Reader 0.2.3). On each load and each new game day, the Reader reads a few facts from the game (league position, the next and last opponents, the head physio and the medical lead) for the other parts. It only reads.
- No new language data: the language is still **English (FM27 Media Mod 2)**, and the builder (1.0.1) is unchanged. Media Lab (0.6.2) and Team Talk Filter (0.3.1) are unchanged.

### Treatment Room 0.6.6-beta (new)

Your medical department, readable. It reads Training > Medical Centre, a player's medical pages and Club > Staff > Medical Team, keeps a record per save, and changes nothing in the game.

- **Guidance from the team:** who's out, who's at risk as far as your department can see, and what's waiting on you. One card for each problem (low condition, low match sharpness, workload and tactical intensity, recurring problems): what it is, who to watch, what the department would do and why, signed by the member of staff who'd give that advice. How far the department sees depends on how good your staff are.
- **Your medical team** at the top, with faces from your facepack if it has them.
- **Sports science:** the latest risk assessment, workload, the season's injuries by month, and how good the flags have been.
- **Briefing, Player files** (each player's record and the physio's view), **Department** (the club's medical staff and their skills, filtered by squad and role, against the division), **Physio's record** (how the estimates held up) and **Diary**.
- A panel on Medical Centre > Season Summary, and a note when a treatment choice lands in your inbox.

### Partnerships 0.3.1-beta (new)

Your player partnerships, readable. It reads the tactics screen, the match screen and the players' happiness pages, keeps a record per save, and changes nothing in the game.

- **This XI:** your XI on a pitch with a line for each partnership, on a seven-step scale built on the game's own bands. For each pair: results when both start and when one plays without the other, the positions they've played together in (and how each went), and who's next when one is missing.
- **Every partnership**, **Coming through** (pairs building an understanding before the game recognises them) and **What the players say**.
- An optional PAIRS button on the tactics pitch that draws the same lines there.

## v0.9.1-beta (22nd November 2026)

- **Language data version 2** (choose **English (FM27 Media Mod 2)** in Preferences > Language):
  - 448 lines that called a named player or manager "they" now use the game's own he/she wording, as SI's originals did ("Every club would want Pablo Felipe after the way he's played").
  - Opposition players are named by surname in the post-match answers ("Morishita was superb", not "Ryoya").
  - Reworded: the "supporters left early" answer that sounded as if you'd scored after they left; "Okay; done."; and one line that read too much like SI's own wording.
- **Press Room voices:** the journalist versions of four questions follow the same wording.
- **Builder 1.0.1:** counts new entries correctly in its log, says "the game's file (SI's, or another mod's)" when it backs up a file another mod has changed, and tells you when an older mod language is still in your languages folder.
- Works alongside mods that replace `simatch.fmf` with different files inside (checked with the Realistic Match Ratings & Smarter AI Selection Mod).
